# AgriNex first farmer pilot

This is a proposed field pilot, not an existing partnership or a claim that farmers have already joined.

## Start with one small produce cluster

Choose one village or nearby market area and one produce category with frequent local demand. Work through a farmer organisation, FPO, cooperative or trusted local agricultural network rather than cold-messaging individual farmers at scale.

Suggested planning cohort:
- 10 farmer participants (target)
- 3 anchor buyers, such as a vegetable shop, restaurant or canteen (target)
- 1 local farmer organisation or trusted coordinator (target)
- 1 produce cluster / delivery zone (target)

These are planning numbers only. Adjust them after conversations with the farmer group.

## Farmer onboarding visit

1. Explain what AgriNex does and what it does not do. Do not promise guaranteed buyers, fixed prices, income increases or instant payouts.
2. Demonstrate phone registration and the pending verification state.
3. Help the farmer enter farm name, village, district and pincode.
4. Verify identity and farm details through the project's chosen lawful process, outside this prototype where necessary.
5. Approve the farmer only after the review is actually completed.
6. Help create one accurate product listing with photo, variety, quantity, unit, harvest date, grade, price and pickup availability.
7. Ask the farmer to confirm that the listing information is correct before publication.

## Buyer-side pilot

1. Onboard a small group of buyers with clear requirements.
2. Start with direct marketplace orders and Cash on Delivery.
3. For larger purchases, let business buyers post a bulk request and compare farmer quotations.
4. Confirm quality, quantity, price, delivery/pickup and cancellation terms before a bulk order is agreed.
5. Do not treat an accepted bulk offer as a completed order; it is a quotation/acceptance step only.

## What to measure

Record weekly, with participant consent:
- Farmers who finish verification and publish at least one product.
- Time required to create and update a listing.
- Listings that receive buyer enquiries and actual orders.
- Completed quantity by produce type.
- Orders cancelled, rejected or delivered.
- Gross value of delivered orders and the farmer payout estimate.
- Whether COD was actually collected (manual confirmation, not inferred from order status).
- Repeat orders from the same buyers.
- Farmer and buyer issues, support requests and reasons for dropping out.

Avoid using app downloads, registrations or listing counts as the main success metric. Track completed transactions and repeat participation.

## Field feedback questions

Ask farmers:
- Which step was difficult: registration, farm verification, product listing, price setting or order handling?
- Would you rather type, speak, or take a photo? Is touch-only input still easy to use?
- Can you reliably receive order updates with your current phone and network?
- Are the displayed charges and estimated earnings understandable?
- What would make you stop using the service?

Ask buyers:
- Was product availability and quality clear?
- Was the price and delivery arrangement agreed before ordering?
- Would you order the same product and quantity again?
- What caused a delay, cancellation or mismatch?

## Pilot safeguards

- Do not collect passwords, OTPs, bank PINs or UPI PINs during field support.
- Do not upload government identity documents until a secure, reviewed KYC workflow is implemented.
- Make all costs and payment arrangements clear before an order is accepted.
- Do not display demo demand, revenue, price or forecast figures as real market data.
- Pause the pilot if inventory, order quantities or payment records cannot be reconciled.
